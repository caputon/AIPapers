The AI Papers

In 1787, partisans for and against a new American constitution argued in print under Roman pseudonyms, forcing each side to articulate first principles instead of slogans. This site stages a similar exchange — not over a constitution, but over the institutions that will govern advanced artificial intelligence. Four regimes. Three questions. Twelve essays and their rebuttals.

Each regime is steelmanned by a pseudonymous advocate. After every essay, rivals respond. The aim is not to decide the question but to make the tradeoffs visible.
