---
title: On the Treaty Timeline and the Concentration Timeline
---

Concord's instruments against transboundary AI concentrations — coordinated antitrust action, multilateral observatories, treaty-based commitments — are instruments I welcome and have, in other domains, endorsed. I observe only that the timeline on which they mature is a timeline that the present concentrations are not waiting on.

The international antitrust coordination Concord describes, in its more developed forms, has been the work of decades. The cross-border merger review protocols among the major jurisdictions were built over a generation. The harmonization of competition policy across the OECD has been an ongoing project since the founding of the organization. The international transparency regimes Concord proposes — modeled on the IAEA — were built in domains where the parties had stronger shared interests in transparency than they currently have in AI. None of this is an objection to the substance of Concord's program. It is an observation about the timeline.

The concentrations the question is asking about are forming on a timeline measured in years. The international substrate against them is being constructed on a timeline measured in decades. The polity that defers its action against the present concentrations until the international substrate matures is the polity that watches the concentrations form unimpeded during the interval, and finds, when the international substrate is finally operational, that the concentrations have settled into configurations the international regime is no longer in a position to address.

I welcome the international substrate. I have urged the firms' participation in its construction. I observe that the present concentrations require action on the present timeline, and that the action available on the present timeline is the lighter action of the company-led regime, applied with discipline and audited externally. The international regime is the long-run amplifier. The present action is the present action.

— *Prometheus*
