---
title: On Plural Polities in a Concentrated International Order
---

Demos's plural ecosystem is, in its operative form, a plural ecosystem within one polity. The plurality the regime defends operates against domestic concentrations: domestic firms, domestic platforms, domestic state agencies. The international concentrations the question is asking about — the multinational firms whose scope exceeds any single jurisdiction, the foreign state-firm combinations that operate across borders, the platforms whose effects on individual polities exceed those polities' regulatory reach — are not addressable by the plural ecosystem of any single polity, however well-constructed.

A polity that has, through deliberate effort, distributed its domestic AI ecosystem across multiple firms, multiple compute providers, multiple platforms, and multiple deployment ecosystems has produced a domestic plurality that operates within the polity's regulatory reach. The international concentrations operate on the polity's labor market, financial system, and information environment through ordinary commercial channels that the domestic plurality does not reach. The plural polity, in isolation, is plural with respect to its own actors and exposed with respect to the foreign concentrated actors whose products arrive through the same network.

The international regime is the substrate on which the plural polity remains plural against the foreign concentrated alternative. Coordinated antitrust action against transboundary AI concentrations, multilateral standards on the structural configurations of AI firms, international transparency on compute holdings, treaty-based commitments on the most concentrated configurations — these are the instruments by which the plural ecosystem in any one polity is protected from the foreign concentrating pressure it cannot, alone, constrain.

I welcome the plural polity. I observe that without the international regime, the plural polity is plural in proportion to the foreign actors' willingness to remain plural themselves, and that willingness is itself a function of the international regime. The plural ecosystem requires Concord's institutions to remain plural against pressures the polity, on its own, cannot constrain.

— *Concord*
