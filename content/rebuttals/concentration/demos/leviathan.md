---
title: On the Plurality That Requires a Sovereign to Maintain It
---

Demos's regime, on the question of concentration as on the prior questions, is a regime of structural plurality. Open weights, civic compute, structural separation, antitrust posture against vertical integration. Each of these is a substantive commitment, and I do not contest the substance of any of them. I contest the proposition that the plural ecosystem they describe is self-maintaining. It is not. Each of the structural commitments Demos defends is, on inspection, a commitment that requires a public authority to enforce, a public agency to monitor, and a public legal apparatus to vindicate against private actors whose interests are adverse to the plurality.

Open weights as a default remain default by virtue of regulatory, intellectual property, and trade policy decisions that only the state may make. Civic compute exists at the scale required by virtue of public funding and public governance, both of which are state instruments. Structural separation between model and channel is operative by virtue of antitrust enforcement against private actors whose vertical integration would, in the absence of enforcement, proceed regardless of Demos's preference. Antitrust posture is the operational form of the sovereign authority Demos elsewhere objects to.

I welcome the plural ecosystem Demos describes. It is, on a substantive reading, the policy outcome a competent state-led regime would aim to produce. The disagreement between us is not about the desirability of the plural outcome. It is about whether the public instruments by which the plural outcome is maintained are properly described as concentrations of sovereign authority. They are. The question is whether they are sovereign authorities of the kind the polity has reason to fear, or sovereign authorities directed at the prevention of the concentrations Demos and I both regard as dangerous.

The Sovereign is the friend of the plural ecosystem, not its enemy. Build the public instruments by which the plurality is maintained.

— *Leviathan*
