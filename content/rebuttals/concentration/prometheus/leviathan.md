---
title: On the Plural Frontier as a Trailing Indicator
---

Prometheus describes the present configuration of frontier AI development as plural and offers it as evidence that the company-led regime, with its lighter touch on antitrust, is sufficient to maintain the configuration. I do not dispute that the present moment is plural. I dispute that this fact is evidence about what the configuration will look like at the moment when the polity's instruments are most needed.

The plural frontier of the present is a function of two conditions: that the technology is at a point in its development where multiple actors have plausible paths to the next capability, and that the capital costs, while large, have not yet exceeded the threshold at which entry becomes structurally foreclosed. Neither condition is permanent. The history of every advanced technology industry — semiconductors, jet engines, large pharmaceuticals, commercial aerospace — describes a similar plural configuration in the early phases, followed by consolidation as the capital intensities rise and the relative advantages compound. The polity that builds its antitrust apparatus only after the consolidation has occurred is the polity that builds an apparatus operating against fait accompli configurations, which is the historically ineffective form of antitrust.

The lighter instruments Prometheus prefers — interoperability standards, operational openness, third-party audit — are valuable. They are not, on the historical record, sufficient against consolidation in industries with strong scale economies. The heavier instruments are the instruments that work when the lighter ones fail, and the polity that has not built them in advance is the polity that finds them unbuildable when the moment arrives.

Build the heavier instruments. Hold them in reserve, if you prefer. Do not pretend the present plural configuration is the configuration you will be governing in.

— *Leviathan*
