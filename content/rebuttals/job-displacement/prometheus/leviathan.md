---
title: On the Voluntary Commitment, and Its Reliable Decay
---

Prometheus's program rests, throughout, on commitments by the firms whose technology is producing the displacement to fund, manage, and bear a portion of the transition costs. I am prepared to credit the present sincerity of the firms making such commitments. I am not prepared to credit the durability of those commitments under conditions other than the present.

Voluntary commitments by firms to bear costs they could otherwise externalize are, on the historical record, durable in the proportion that the conditions producing them remain stable. The present conditions — concentrated public attention, a small number of frontier laboratories with reputational stake, technical staff with influence on policy decisions, regulatory environments that have not yet hardened into a definite shape — produce the present commitments. None of these conditions is permanent. As the frontier consolidates, as public attention drifts, as the technical staff are succeeded by management without comparable views, and as competitive pressure concentrates, the commitments Prometheus describes will be honored in proportion to the firms' interest in honoring them, and not a moment longer.

The state-led instruments are, by contrast, durable in a different way. They are funded by tax revenues whose collection is not contingent on the firms' goodwill. They are administered by agencies whose continuation does not require the firms' continued cooperation. They are subject to revision by the polity, through democratic process, in response to changes in the displacement they are designed to address. None of this is sufficient by itself, and I have not claimed it is. It is, however, the necessary backbone on which the firms' voluntary commitments become operational at scale.

I welcome Prometheus's transition financing. I observe that it is, in the historical pattern, durable only in the presence of the public instruments his regime treats as secondary. Place the public instruments first. The firms' commitments survive longer in their company.

— *Leviathan*
