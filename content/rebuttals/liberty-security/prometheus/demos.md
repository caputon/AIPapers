---
title: On the Closure of the Open Window
---

Prometheus is correct that much excellent safety work has come from the laboratories. He is silent on why. The window in which the laboratories produced credible safety work has been a window of plural competition among rivals genuinely uncertain who would lead, of public attention sufficient to make reputational concerns operative, and of a sense among technical staff that they were being watched by peers and citizens who would notice if commitments were quietly relaxed. Each of these conditions is contingent on a plural AI ecosystem.

The company-led regime, generalized into the steady-state arrangement Prometheus prefers, would close the window from which it draws its evidence. As the frontier consolidates — as the costs of competing rise, as the watchers tire, as the technical staff age into management or move into the regulatory bodies they once dealt with — the discipline of the makers will not survive the conditions that produced it. This is not a hypothetical projection. It is the trajectory of every concentrated industry that began with public-spirited founders and ended with regulatory capture.

The decentralized regime is not the absence of safety work. It is the structural condition under which safety work continues to be done credibly. Open evaluations performed by parties with no financial interest in the conclusions. Civic compute resources sufficient to permit independent investigation. Antitrust posture sufficient to keep the laboratories competitively distinct. Without these, Prometheus's discipline is the discipline of the present moment, and the present moment is not the moment we will be governing in.

— *Demos*
