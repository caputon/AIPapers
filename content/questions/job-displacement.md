---
title: On the Question of Work and Displacement
---

The question of work has shadowed every major industrial transition. It returns now in a sharper form, because the technology under discussion is not narrowly automating; it is broadly substituting, across cognitive labor that has historically been the route by which most people earn a livelihood and most societies legitimate their unequal distributions of wealth.

A governance regime that fails to address displacement is not, in the political sense, a governance regime at all. The question is not whether to address it but who acts, on what authority, with what tools, and at whose expense. The regimes differ on every one of these questions.
