---
title: On the Question of Liberty and Security
---

Of the questions before us, this one is the oldest. It is the question Hobbes asked of the sovereign, Madison of the Constitution, and every generation since of the technology of its day. We ask it now of artificial intelligence because the answer is no longer obvious.

Frontier AI poses a class of risks that the framers of our existing liberties did not contemplate: capabilities that, in the wrong hands, could lower the cost of biological catastrophe, undermine democratic deliberation at scale, or produce autonomous behavior whose consequences exceed the foresight of their authors. To do nothing is to wager that none of these risks materializes. To do everything one might wish is to construct an apparatus of supervision incompatible with a free society.

Each regime answers in its own voice.
